# Compelling Design Gate

Read before concept generation and at the early native proof and full-design reviews. Correct spacing and working controls establish competence, not character. The goal is a particular editorial experience that remains recognizable with different content and interesting without exhausting its readers.

## Give The Publication A Point Of View

Write one sentence connecting a reader's reason to return, the author's actual publishing habit, and an organizing idea. A palette, illustration style, or fashionable font pairing is not that idea. Translate it into visible decisions about reading, browsing, chronology, captions, or the relationship between long essays and short notes. Do not invent factual author claims or require bespoke writing/art for every post.

Make the signature structural: a memorable type relationship, composition, content rhythm, or useful browsing treatment expressed through native editable blocks and shared styles. Artwork supports that system rather than sitting above an otherwise interchangeable feed. Preserve semantic reading order and ordinary WordPress behavior.

## Compose Rhythm, Not Repeated Decoration

Before rendering, compare each concept's content map and reader journey without its colors, fonts, or artwork. If all three reduce to masthead, featured essay, note, photo, and archive in the same order, they are one feed with different styling: replace the premises before spending on images. Specify a different organizing relationship or browsing model for each, then make that relationship visibly legible in the actual composition and phone adaptation.

Invent a reading experience, not merely a louder feed. Collections of fragments, connected reflections over time, or issue-like editorial arrangements are possible lenses, not required themes. Ordinary posts must still work without mandatory manual curation, fabricated claims, bespoke demo content, or particular photos. Normal author-written reflections are valid optional content. If an advanced relationship needs optional author input, expose that cost and provide a coherent automatic fallback; do not invent related-post intelligence or WordPress capabilities.

Use a mental monochrome and missing-media test: what remains recognizable beyond the accent color, oversized title, and decorative image? This concept-stage reasoning does not replace the actual missing-media fixture in native proof. More color, type scale, texture, or illustration is not evidence that a rejected premise has changed. Review the rendered result against its promised content map; a model that defaults back to familiar rows has not fulfilled the concept, regardless of polish. On phone, preserve the organizing idea rather than retaining only the palette.

Choose where the page is emphatic and where it becomes quiet. Essays, brief observations, and photographic entries can have different useful treatments without becoming unrelated designs or equal-sized cards. Use optional native patterns and supported dynamic layouts; do not require hand-curating every entry, fake classifications, or particular images for the default theme to work.

Develop a few precise details that reinforce the idea: an article opening, caption relationship, archive grouping, or transition from an essay to the next reading destination. Do not add all of them as a checklist. Functional delight need not be animation, extra controls, slogans, or ornament in every gap. Quiet, exuberant, dense, and sparse designs can each be compelling; this gate does not prescribe one house style.

## Judge The Actual Concepts

### Be Adventurous In The Concept, Precise In Execution

Develop three different creative bets, not three skins: explore typography, composition, and an unexpected editorial organizing idea as different dominant lenses. Lenses may overlap or be replaced when the brief warrants it; they are not prescribed visual styles or a recurring formula. Each concept must differ in actual structure, take one deliberate risk, and explain its reader benefit, possible cost, and what must be proved later. Larger logos, louder colors, and arbitrary decoration alone do not count as risks.

Separate exploration from implementation validation. Accessibility, readable text, semantic intent, and recognizable controls remain mandatory; do not require every early sketch to be production-resolved or suppress promising ideas solely because the native implementation needs investigation. Record uncertain WordPress capabilities as hypotheses, not promises. After selection, resolve those uncertainties through the bounded native proof before expanding the theme.

Ask Marge: "What would someone remember tomorrow?" and "Could this pass for a stock theme?" Reject attractive-but-forgettable premises with a visible reason; never equate novelty with quality or claim exhaustive uniqueness. Preserve useful oddness rather than averaging the alternatives into the safest layout.

Before building the selected direction, generate one more daring version of its strongest idea within an explicit generation budget. Compare the actual versions, decide what to retain or pull back for reading and use, and confirm the direction with the user unless selection was delegated. This is one focused exploration, not another three-concept round or an open-ended polishing loop.

Present exactly three numbered alternatives with comparable realistic content and materially different structures. Include a text-only entry, ordinary unmatched media, and long copy. For each record: editorial idea, visible signature, native implementation surface, phone adaptation, and honest tradeoff.

Ask the independent reviewer using Marge and Impeccable to answer from actual images:
- What will a reader remember beyond the logo, color, or decorative image?
- Which visible relationship belongs to this publication rather than its category's default?
- Do text-only and ordinary-photo entries retain that identity?
- Where does the composition reward attention, and where does it allow comfortable reading?
- What is merely competent, what is distinctive, and what remains weak?

Separate those taste judgments from readability, fidelity, and functional evidence. Describe observed details; do not claim uniqueness, audience consensus, or exceptional quality without supporting evidence. A tidy page is not automatically an appealing one, and a strange page is not automatically good.

## Refuse Weak Premises Early

If none of the concepts has a convincing editorial idea expressed in its structure, do not force a winner or commission all screens. Explain the visible weakness and revise the premise within the agreed generation budget. A user may still explicitly choose a quiet or familiar direction; preserve that choice and its stated tradeoff rather than overruling it.

After selection, carry the distinctive relationships into the small native home/article/menu proof before expansion. Replace editorial photos or remove them in a safe fixture; verify the signature survives with real content and remains editable. Fix structural genericness through type, composition, and block relationships, not by ordering prettier photos or more art.

At the full mockup and browser reviews, explicitly compare those same signature decisions with the selected target. A build that retains colors while losing editorial rhythm has failed fidelity even if it functions. Reuse evidence and one bounded correction pass; never turn this gate into an endless search for a subjective perfect score.
